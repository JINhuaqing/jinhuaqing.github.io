I am **Huaqing Jin**. I am an [assistant professor](https://www.stat.tsinghua.edu.cn/en/info/1023/1475.htm) in the [Department of Statistics and Data Science](https://www.stat.tsinghua.edu.cn/en/info/1023/1475.htm) at Tsinghua University (since September 2025).

Before that, I was a postdoctoral scholar in the Department of Epidemiology and Biostatistics at UCSF (March 2024–September 2025), supervised by Dr. [Fei Jiang](https://jianglab.ucsf.edu/home) and Prof. [Chiung-Yu Huang](https://profiles.ucsf.edu/chiung-yu.huang). Earlier, I was a postdoctoral scholar in computational neuroscience at UCSF (July 2022–March 2025), working with Dr. [Fei Jiang](https://jianglab.ucsf.edu/home) and Prof. [Ashish Raj](https://radiology.ucsf.edu/people/ashish-raj) in the [Brain Networks Laboratory](https://radiology.ucsf.edu/research/labs/brain-networks-lab) with Prof. [Srikantan Nagarajan](https://profiles.ucsf.edu/srikantan.nagarajan).

I obtained my PhD in statistics under Dr. [Fei Jiang](https://jianglab.ucsf.edu/home) and Prof. [Guosheng Yin](https://web.hku.hk/~gyin/) at the University of Hong Kong (2018–2022) and my B.S. in statistics from Zhejiang University (2014–2018).


## Research interests

**Methodologies:** Bayesian methods, high-dimensional statistics, change-point detection, deep generative models, conformal prediction.

**Application areas:** Neuroimaging data analysis in Alzheimer's disease; Bayesian clinical trial design in oncology.


## News

* 2025-11: Our paper "_High-dimensional inference for functional regression with an application to the Alzheimer's disease magnetoencephalography study_" has been accepted for publication in ***Biostatistics***. 

* 2025-09: I joined the Department of Statistics and Data Science at Tsinghua University as a tenure-track assistant professor.
